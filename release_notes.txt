v3.1.3 - Notification Engine Replacement

Swapped flutter_local_notifications for awesome_notifications.

WHY: flutter_local_notifications relies on Android AlarmManager which
is unreliable — Android kills scheduled alarms when app is backgrounded.

awesome_notifications uses foreground service-based scheduling which
Android cannot kill. It also handles boot rescheduling automatically.

CHANGES:
- New notification engine: awesome_notifications 0.12.1
- Foreground service-based delivery (not AlarmManager)
- Automatic rescheduling on device reboot
- Built-in POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission handling
- WakeUpScreen for immediate visibility
- preciseAlarm for exact timing
